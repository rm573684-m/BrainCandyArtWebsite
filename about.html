About Brain Candy Art

The Project

Brain Candy Art is a surreal collage project blending layered textures, emotional storytelling, and bold yet human color. Each piece reflects an energy shift — from joy and chaos to memory, dream logic, and absurdity.

The Process

The work draws inspiration from vintage print media, everyday objects, and spontaneous fragments of imagery, all assembled to form new, often unexpected, narratives. Our process is iterative and focused on capturing a singular, strong feeling.

The Artist

Maura Lucchese grew up in Cocoa Beach, and was mentored by her father, a senior design engineer for the Space Program. As a scientist, he encouraged her to push herself to excellence in art in order to keep her active mind busy. In school, she was taught by RL Lewis, a famous Florida Highwayman painter. Before starting a family, she created Maura’s Magic Marble Candles which achieved a cult following with the rock stars of the ‘90s Grunge era in Seattle and were sold out regularly in galleries. Later, several mind-altering tragedies which garnered public interest, brought Maura to a precipice and her only options were to give up or use the setbacks as a springboard to inspire others. She chose the latter. In the midst of the public eye, she recovered from a brain and spinal cord injury. She dealt with her grief by making meticulous collages, which take between 350-400 hours of work. These are made from only recycled magazines and varnish on stretched canvas or occasionally wood. Her unique, emotional, and very personal portraits have been noticed by art lovers worldwide. Her philosophy is to use the full complement of human emotion as an agent for positive change, negating labels meant to control vulnerable individuals or groups, to expose the abuse of people and our planet, and to effectively educate, entertain, and edify a culture where pro-social behavior is the norm, not the exception.
